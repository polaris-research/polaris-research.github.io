Concept Shift

Re-defining Publicness for Platforms
01. The Arena
Physical Space → Digital Artifact
Publics no longer gather in squares, but around specific objects (posts, videos, tweets).
02. Interaction Mode
Proximity → Digital Trace
Friction between bodies is replaced by signals (likes, shares) mediated by algorithms.
03. Temporality
Fleeting → Persistent
Encounters are not ephemeral; they are recorded, stored, and searchable asynchronously.
